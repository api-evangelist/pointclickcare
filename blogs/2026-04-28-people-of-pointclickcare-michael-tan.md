</ul><h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a-journey-rooted-in-discipline">A Journey Rooted in Discipline</h3><p>Michael grew up in the suburbs outside Ottawa as the son of immigrant parents&mdash;his father from Singapore, his mother from China. His childhood was filled with diverse pursuits that shaped his future in unexpected ways.</p><p>&ldquo;I grew up doing a lot of extracurricular activities,&rdquo; Michael recalls. &ldquo;I was classically trained in piano and deeply involved in music programs throughout high school.&rdquo; These experiences taught him discipline and attention to detail&mdash;skills that would prove invaluable in his finance career.</p><p>After always having an interest in going into business Michael completed his undergraduate degree at Queen&rsquo;s University and earned his CPA designation. He started his career with prestigious firms like Deloitte and KPMG before pivoting &ldquo;out of large accounting firms and into private companies,&rdquo; including a small software company.</p><p>The opportunity to join PCC came through a contract position listing. &ldquo;I happened to stumble upon a listing for a contract position with PCC in accounting and revenue,&rdquo; Michael remembers. &ldquo;And that&rsquo;s what brought me here and I&rsquo;ve loved it ever since.&rdquo;</p><div class="wp-block-image mt-4 mb-4">
<figure class="aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-64481" height="767" src="https://pointclickcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Michael-Tan-blog-photo-03-1024x767.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>
</div><h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-making-an-impact-at-pcc">Making an Impact at PCC</h3><p>Today, Michael serves as Senior Revenue Analyst, focusing on revenue accounting and managing relationships with technology partners. Beyond his core responsibilities, he&rsquo;s involved in strategic initiatives that keep him engaged and challenged.</p><p>But it&rsquo;s Michael&rsquo;s involvement in the Change Agent Network at PCC that his collaborative spirit truly shines. &ldquo;The objective of the network is to have a cross-functional group of people from every department participate in a monthly connect to speak cross functionally,&rdquo; he explains, &ldquo;so that we can enable and make change happen faster.&rdquo;</p><p>This collaborative approach embodies what makes PCC special: bringing together diverse perspectives to drive meaningful change across the organization.</p><h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-power-of-great-people">The Power of Great People</h3><p>When asked what&rsquo;s most rewarding about working at PointClickCare, Michael&rsquo;s answer is immediate. &ldquo;Right now, what&rsquo;s most rewarding is the people I&rsquo;m working with. I&rsquo;ve got an amazing team to turn to for support and guidance when things start to get chaotic and knowing they&rsquo;re always willing to help makes all the difference.&rdquo;</p><p>This supportive team dynamic isn&rsquo;t an accident&mdash;it&rsquo;s by design. Support, guidance, and teamwork have been embedded into the DNA of PointClickCare since its inception. Michael says that the culture at PCC is unlike any other he&rsquo;s experienced, particularly in how leadership balances ambition with humanity. &ldquo;Leadership keeps us on a high performance trajectory while maintaining that culture of&mdash;we&rsquo;ve got a bigger purpose here and work isn&rsquo;t everything. We have to enjoy life too,&rdquo; he explains. &ldquo;The culture at PCC is very unique in that the opportunities for growth aren&rsquo;t just encouraged, they are nurtured, honed, and developed. The leadership team is dedicated to fostering an environment where people can thrive, both professionally and personally.&rdquo;</p><div class="wp-block-image mb-4 mt-4">
<figure class="aligncenter size-large"><img alt="" class="wp-image-64480" height="768" src="https://pointclickcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Michael-Tan-blog-photo-02-1024x768.jpg" width="1024" /></figure>
</div><h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-beyond-the-traditional-metrics">Beyond the Traditional Metrics</h3><p>When it comes to defining success, Michael takes a thoughtful approach. &ldquo;I try not to define success too much by hard milestones or a certain level of income or being at a certain stage in my career. Everyone&rsquo;s got different circumstances and things that happen in their life that put them in different places.&rdquo;</p><p>For Michael, success is less about reaching predetermined benchmarks and more about continuous growth, contributing meaningfully to his team, and finding fulfillment in the work itself. It&rsquo;s a mindset that keeps him grounded and focused on what truly matters.</p><h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-makes-pcc-different">What Makes PCC Different</h3><p>To Michael, what makes PCC different comes down to a rare achievement: the company has scaled from a small startup into a rapidly growing enterprise without losing its soul. &ldquo;PCC started out as a very small organization but grew very rapidly,&rdquo; he explains. &ldquo;But what&rsquo;s amazing is that somehow it has managed, despite all the growth, to maintain that really small, tight-knit family feel. It&rsquo;s the best of both worlds&mdash;the resources and opportunities of a big company with the supportive leadership, collaborative culture, and genuine care of a close-knit team.&rdquo; That combination has made Michael a devoted member of the PCC community.</p><p>Thank you for sharing your story with us, Michael! Your blend of technical expertise, collaborative spirit, and authentic personality exemplifies what makes the PointClickCare community so special.</p><div class="pcc-theme-block position-relative call-to-action-block component-theme-light  background-cover-image font-weight-light" id="call-to-action-block_6d5926a0d5bfbb55647a6fd7bba30cb5"><!-- font-weight:300 via Bootstrap utility -->
