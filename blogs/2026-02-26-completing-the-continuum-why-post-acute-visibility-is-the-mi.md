<p>Most health plans have made meaningful progress improving visibility into acute care. Care managers can see hospital admissions as they happen, identify high-risk members, and intervene during critical moments. These investments are paying dividends&mdash;yet many organizations still face stubbornly high readmissions, rising post-acute costs, and growing pressure on quality performance.</p><p>The question executives are increasingly asking is not <strong>whether</strong> their care management strategy is sound&mdash;<strong>but why outcomes still fall short</strong>.</p><p>The answer is rarely a people or process problem. It&rsquo;s a visibility problem.</p><p>Specifically, a gap in insight that <a>emerges</a> the moment a member leaves acute care.</p><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-visibility-gap-hiding-in-plain-sight">The Visibility Gap Hiding in Plain Sight</h4><p>Healthcare delivery does not end at discharge&mdash;but visibility often does.</p><p>Once a member transitions into a skilled nursing facility (SNF) or long-term care (LTC) setting, health plans lose real-time awareness of what happens next. Clinical updates stall. Status changes go unseen. Emerging risks surface only after the fact&mdash;often when a readmission has already occurred.</p><p>This is not the result of poor strategy or underinvestment. It&rsquo;s the byproduct of infrastructure that was never designed for real-time coordination across the continuum.</p><p>Claims arrive weeks later. Status checks require phone calls and manual follow-up. Retrospective reports provide insight into what happened&mdash;but not <em>when intervention could have changed the outcome.</em></p><p>The result is an incomplete view of the member journey at precisely the point where risk compounds.</p><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-why-post-acute-blind-spots-drive-readmissions">Why Post-Acute Blind Spots Drive Readmissions</h4><p>The post-acute phase is where complexity accelerates&mdash;and where most health plans have the least visibility.</p><p>Consider the data:</p><ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Post-acute care represents <strong>more than $359B in annual spend</strong></li>



<li><strong>$4.34B</strong> is attributed specifically to SNF-related readmissions</li>



<li><strong>78% of those readmissions are considered avoidable</strong></li>



<li><strong>73% of Medicare spending variation occurs in post-acute settings</strong>, not hospitals</li>
</ul><p>(Source: CMS ACO Quality Initiatives &amp; Patient Assessment Instruments)</p><p>Yet, based on a recent study in collaboration with <a href="https://sage-growth.com/">Sage Growth Partners</a>, we discovered only <strong>14% of health plans report having real-time visibility</strong> into SNF and LTC care.</p><p>That mismatch matters.</p><p>Without timely insight, predictable breakdowns occur:</p><ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Medication changes aren&rsquo;t reconciled across settings</li>



<li>Discharge plans lose continuity</li>



<li>Early signs of decline&mdash;wounds, infections, functional changes&mdash;go unnoticed</li>



<li>Follow-up becomes reactive instead of intentional</li>
</ul><p>By the time claims or retrospective data surface these issues, the opportunity to intervene has often passed.</p><p>This is not a marginal inefficiency. It&rsquo;s a primary driver of avoidable cost, quality risk, and member dissatisfaction.</p><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-shift-from-utilization-control-to-outcome-accountability">The Shift from Utilization Control to Outcome Accountability</h4><p>Traditional utilization management still plays an important role&mdash;but its influence diminishes once a member enters post-acute care.</p><p>At the same time, quality performance is becoming more financially consequential. Measures like <strong>Plan All-Cause Readmissions</strong>, now triple-weighted for Medicare Advantage plan, directly impact revenue. Compressed authorization timelines and regulatory scrutiny limit how much UM alone can move the needle.</p><p>As a result, care management&mdash;not utilization control&mdash;has become the primary lever for improving outcomes.</p><p>But care management only works when teams can see what&rsquo;s happening.</p><p>Without real-time post-acute insight, even the best care managers are forced to respond after risks have already escalated.</p><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-from-retrospective-reporting-to-live-clinical-intelligence">From Retrospective Reporting to Live Clinical Intelligence</h4><p>Most data solutions still operate after the fact. They aggregate information for reporting, analytics, and quality measurement&mdash;important functions, but insufficient for time-sensitive intervention.</p><p>What&rsquo;s changed is the ability to connect care settings <em>as care is delivered.</em></p><p>Our nationwide, EHR-agnostic care collaboration network now makes real-time post-acute visibility and intelligence possible&mdash;linking hospitals, SNFs, LTC facilities, and ambulatory settings into a shared clinical fabric.</p><p>When a member transitions from hospital to SNF:</p><ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Care teams receive immediate clinical context</li>



<li>Status changes are visible as they occur</li>



<li>Early warning signals become actionable&mdash;not historical</li>
</ul><p>This shift fundamentally changes execution:</p><ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>SNF stays can be monitored in real time</li>



<li>Medication reconciliation becomes consistent</li>



<li>Discharge planning becomes proactive</li>



<li>Follow-up is timely, targeted, and coordinated</li>
</ul><p>The difference is not more data&mdash;it&rsquo;s better timing.</p><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-what-changes-when-care-managers-can-see-the-full-journey">What Changes When Care Managers Can See the Full Journey</h4><p>Health plans have already proven that real-time intelligence improves outcomes in acute care. Extending that same visibility into post-acute settings is the logical next step.</p><p>It doesn&rsquo;t replace existing systems. It completes them.</p><p>With full-continuum insight, care managers can answer questions that today require manual outreach:</p><ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Where is the member now?</li>



<li>What is their current clinical status?</li>



<li>Who is trending toward readmission risk?</li>



<li>When is safe discharge likely?</li>
</ul><p>Plans that close this visibility gap are seeing tangible results:</p><ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Fewer preventable readmissions</li>



<li>More efficient care management workflows</li>



<li>Improved Stars and HEDIS performance</li>



<li>Stronger margin protection</li>
</ul><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-a-capability-few-have-today-but-most-will-need-tomorrow">A Capability Few Have Today&mdash;But Most Will Need Tomorrow</h4><p>Today, most health plans still lack real-time post-acute visibility&mdash;but that is changing.</p><p>As quality thresholds tighten and revenue becomes increasingly outcome-dependent, this capability is quickly shifting from differentiator to expectation.</p><p>Organizations that act now gain an advantage others will be forced to catch up to later.</p><h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-the-bottom-line">The Bottom Line</h4><p>The largest drivers of cost variation and readmission risk sit in the least visible part of the care continuum.</p><p>Forward-thinking health plans are closing that gap&mdash;connecting acute and post-acute intelligence to enable earlier intervention, stronger coordination, and better outcomes.</p><p>The infrastructure exists.<br />The opportunity is measurable. <br />And the organizations that complete the continuum will be best positioned to lead in the next phase of care management performance.</p><p>Learn how your organization can achieve similar results at <a href="https://pointclickcare.com/products/pac-management-health-plans" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">pointclickcare.com/products/pac-management-health-plans</a>.</p><div class="pcc-theme-block position-relative form-block background-cover-image  block-vertical-margin  background-color-white2  component-theme-white2" id="form-block-row-block_2de3cbbe0d551aa2a02e80896dd9f3ba">
