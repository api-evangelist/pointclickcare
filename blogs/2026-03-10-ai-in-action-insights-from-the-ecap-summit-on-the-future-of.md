<p>The conversation around AI in skilled nursing has officially shifted from theoretical &ldquo;what-ifs&rdquo; to real-world results. At the recent 2026 eCap Summit, the industry&rsquo;s most forward-thinking leaders gathered to address a critical reality: sustaining high-quality care amidst a mounting workforce crisis requires more than just innovation&mdash;it requires application embedded in workflow.</p><p>As a leader at the center of these dialogues, PointClickCare is moving the needle from concept to adoption, proving that when AI is purposefully embedded within the clinician&rsquo;s workflow, it restores the capacity to care.</p><div class="wp-block-image mt-4 mb-4">
<figure class="aligncenter size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-63975" height="800" src="https://pointclickcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26B13_D2_Reinventing_Skills_SM-4995-CC.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>
</div><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Landscape: Beyond the AI Hype</h3><p>At the eCap Summit, the atmosphere among skilled nursing leaders was one of focused urgency. While AI&rsquo;s visibility has been increasing for years, the dialogue has matured. Leaders are no longer interested in the abstract potential of technology; they are looking for immediate solutions to the compounding pressures of the workforce crisis, rising clinical complexity, and tightening margins.</p><p>The core challenge discussed wasn&rsquo;t just about doing more with less&mdash;it was about returning time to clinicians. The industry is reaching a tipping point where manual documentation and reactive care models are no longer sustainable. To thrive, providers must shift toward a predictive model that identifies risks before they become emergencies.</p><p>At the heart of these conversations was a clear message: AI shouldn&rsquo;t be an extra task for staff. Instead, it must be the invisible engine that quietly unifies data and automates the administrative friction that leads to burnout. By moving AI from a high-level concept to a foundational layer of the EHR, we are helping providers bridge the gap between surviving the day and strategically scaling for the future.</p><div class="wp-block-image mt-4 mb-4">
<figure class="aligncenter size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-63976" height="800" src="https://pointclickcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26B13_D2_Aft_Party_PE-4539-CC.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>
</div><h3 class="wp-block-heading">Reinventing Skilled Nursing Through AI</h3><p>One of the most anticipated moments of the eCap Summit was the session, &ldquo;Reinventing Skilled Nursing Through AI,&rdquo; featuring PointClickCare Chief Product and Technology Officer David Pessis and Apploi&rsquo;s Chief Technology Officer Will Plourde. The discussion transcended technical specifications of AI, focusing instead on putting the human in the loop with technology.</p><p>The PointClickCare and Apploi partnership also highlights another critical reality: solving the workforce crisis is a key enabler of AI adoption. By achieving operational stability through streamlining staffing and onboarding, SNFs can gain more breathing room, helping them to more successfully implement and scale advanced AI across their organizations.</p><p>In the discussion, David emphasized a critical point: for AI to be successful in a high-stakes environment like skilled nursing, it cannot be &ldquo;just another system.&rdquo; It shouldn&rsquo;t be a destination clinicians have to visit; it should be an intelligence that meets them where they already work&mdash;within the EHR.</p><p>This approach is rooted in transparency and accountability, focusing on delivering early, meaningful wins&mdash;such as streamlined documentation and improved survey readiness&mdash;that build staff confidence and prove that AI is there to empower clinicians, not replace them.</p><p>The session broke down the real-world impact of AI into four key categories:</p><ul class="wp-block-list" style="font-size: 1.13rem;">
<li><strong>Clinical Outcomes:</strong>&nbsp;Moving the needle on reduced readmissions and fall risk trends by identifying subtle changes in resident status.</li>



<li><strong>Operational Efficiency:</strong>&nbsp;Improving documentation completeness and smoothing care transitions, which directly leads to fewer survey deficiencies.</li>



<li><strong>Workforce Impact:</strong>&nbsp;Decreasing the administrative burden to boost staff satisfaction and give caregivers more time at the bedside.</li>



<li><strong>Financial Sustainability:</strong>&nbsp;Ensuring reimbursement accuracy and reducing the costly overhead associated with adverse events.</li>
</ul><p>The takeaway was clear: PointClickCare isn&rsquo;t just experimenting with AI; we are deeply focused on scaling for impact. As David shared, the goal is to provide a predictive safety net that empowers teams to make intentional, data-driven decisions that improve both the resident experience and the facility&rsquo;s bottom line.</p><div class="wp-block-image mt-4 mb-4">
<figure class="aligncenter size-full"><img alt="" class="wp-image-63977" height="800" src="https://pointclickcare.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/26B13_D2_Gala_SM-5729-CC.jpg" width="1200" /></figure>
</div><h3 class="wp-block-heading">The Path Forward: From Reactive Care to a Co-Pilot Future</h3><p>As the eCap Summit made clear, the industry is at a pivotal crossroads. When we look at the two-to-three-year outlook for skilled nursing, it is evident that the era of &ldquo;reactive&rdquo; management&mdash;where teams move from one crisis to the next&mdash;is coming to an end.</p><div class="pcc-theme-block position-relative wysiwyg-block " id="wysiwyg-block_476d28b6bbe0b4ee2b6687f67a612d88">
