<p>In today’s fast-paced world, technology is always changing. Artificial intelligence (AI) is one of the most exciting and transformative innovations shaping the workplace. As we stand on the cusp of a new era, AI is more than just a buzzword—it’s a powerful tool changing how we work, think, and innovate.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-taking-a-stance-we-re-all-in">Taking a Stance: We’re All In</h4>



<p>At PointClickCare (PCC), we empower our people to be the future architects of a smarter healthcare system—one that is human-first and accelerated by AI to create meaningful and lasting change.</p>



<p>Our employees harness AI as a catalyst for creativity, productivity, and thoughtful decision-making. By integrating AI tools into daily workflows, collaboration is enhanced, outcomes are improved, and every team member can maximize their impact.</p>



<p>It all starts with our hiring practices, where we explore the AI fluency of candidates and source specific AI expertise to complement our mission. Job descriptions have also been updated to reflect AI accountabilities. Plus, we have implemented AI-powered talent acquisition sourcing and selection tools so we can find the right people, for the right roles, faster. &nbsp;We then continue to invest in training and development to nurture innovation throughout the employee journey.</p>



<p>To help our people embrace these capabilities, we’re expanding access to training and upskilling so employees in a variety of roles can build AI knowledge and confidence. For example, all employees are required to complete AI fluency foundations courses to ensure a baseline understanding of AI. In addition, we hosted our first ever AI symposium, a company-wide event showcasing how employees are applying AI in their everyday work, and we plan to keep that momentum going into 2026. Our goal is to make AI a shared capability that empowers more of us to innovate, adapt, and lead in a rapidly evolving world.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-embracing-ai-in-healthcare-the-first-step-to-workforce-empowerment">Embracing AI in Healthcare: The First Step to Workforce Empowerment</h4>



<p>The journey to empowerment starts with a single step, and in the world of AI, that step is embracing the technology. For many organizations, integrating AI may seem overwhelming, but the rewards are worth it.</p>



<p>Some of the most significant benefits include:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list" style="font-size: 1.13rem;">
<li>Simplifying repetitive tasks</li>



<li>Providing real-time insights</li>



<li>Freeing teams to focus on creative problem-solving and strategic planning</li>



<li>Improving job satisfaction by reducing tedious work</li>
</ul>



<p>Successful AI integration isn’t just about technology—it’s about people. That’s why company-wide education and open communication are critical. Leaders play a key role by modeling how AI can be valuable. When leaders embrace AI, they set the stage for a culture of innovation and adaptability, accelerating adoption across the organization.</p>



<p>What is also critical is coming up with an AI people framework that looks at functions, role types, and the AI acumen and skill type that is, or will be, required in each of those functions and roles (i.e., does a specific role need to be AI-Aware, AI-Augmented, or AI-Powered).&nbsp; We are actively working with our leaders on that framework, which will also help us come up with tailored learning paths.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-ai-in-healthcare-real-world-applications-that-empower-teams">AI in Healthcare: Real-World Applications That Empower Teams</h4>



<p>From predictive analytics to automated processes, AI is a game-changer with practical applications that revolutionize how teams work.</p>



<p>At PCC, we’re proud to shine a light on the successful AI adoption we’ve experienced across PointClickCare teams, including:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list" style="font-size: 1.13rem;">
<li>AI-powered assistants (Claude Code and Copilot) and automations that reduce toil, enabling faster coding, smarter test creation, automated security reviews, accelerated incident RCA, and modernization of legacy systems. These tools also enhance the customer support experience and help identify customer risks and trends.</li>



<li>AI‑driven categorization and automated routing ensure support inquiries reach the right teams quickly and efficiently.</li>



<li>Transformation of customer insights into presentation-ready data.</li>



<li>Autonomous handling of security alerts and remediation within our environment.</li>
</ul>



<p>These are just some examples of internal AI-driven solutions that improve efficiency, reduce complexity, and strengthen customer conversations—all while empowering employees.</p>



<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="h-looking-forward-shaping-the-future-of-work-with-ai">Looking Forward: Shaping the Future of Work with AI</h4>



<p>The future of work is not about humans versus machines, it’s about humans using technology responsibly to augment our work in a meaningful way.</p>



<p>By embracing AI and leveraging its capabilities, organizations can unlock human potential, drive innovation, and achieve greater success. At PointClickCare, we are committed to harnessing AI to empower our people and shape the future of healthcare.</p>
